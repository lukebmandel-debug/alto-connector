# Alto Privacy Policy (closed beta)
Version 1.0. Last updated 3 November 2026.

## Who we are
Alto is a study tool that turns course notes into timelines, outlines and memory palaces. During the closed beta it is run by Luke Mandel, an individual ("we"). Questions, requests and complaints: lukebmandel@gmail.com.

## What we collect
- **Your account.** When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID, and we record when you sign in.
- **Your content.** The notes, pages, timelines, cards, highlights, study progress, files you attach, backgrounds you import and settings you create or upload.
- **Items you share.** Anything you share by link can be opened by anyone who has the link, until you turn the link off.
- **Bass (our AI builder), if you use it.** Each request sends Anthropic, which runs the Claude AI model: our instructions to Bass, the text and files you submit, the conversation so far, the parts of your timeline Bass reads, and Bass's replies. It does not send your name, email or account ID. What Bass builds is stored as your content. The live conversation stays in our server's memory until that server restarts. We keep a record of each request: time, credits used, action and status, not the text. If you choose Help improve Bass (off unless you tick it), we also save your typed messages and Bass's replies, not the files you add; see Bass conversations you share.
- **Bass conversations you share (optional).** If you opt in, we save your messages to Bass and its replies under your account, and Luke Mandel and people he asks to help, including AI tools, may read them to improve Bass. We remove your account ID before analysis. We keep them until 15 February 2027, or until you stop sharing or delete your account, and delete them within 7 days of either. You can stop at any time in Bass settings or by email. Please do not type other people's private information into Bass.
- **Documents you add to Bass.** The text of files you add (PDF, Word, text) is stored in our database under your account. We do not keep the original file. When Bass uses a document, its text is sent to Anthropic with your request, like anything else you submit to Bass. Deleting a document hides it at once and erases it from our storage within 7 days; Anthropic keeps what it received for up to 30 days.
- **Technical records.** Our server logs (time, account ID, action; IP address and browser type) and security logs of refused access (including IP address) are kept for 30 days. Google's hosting servers also see your IP address and browser when your browser loads Alto, under Google's own policies.
- **Your agreement.** When you accept the Beta Terms we record your account ID, the version, the time and that you confirmed you are 18 or over.
- **Feedback.** If you use our feedback form (a Google Form), Google records the name, email address and profile photo of your Google account when you submit it or upload a file, and we receive those along with what you type and any file you attach. Google handles the form under its own privacy policy.
- **Tester list.** The email addresses of invited testers.

## What we do not do
We do not show ads, sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use tracking cookies or third-party analytics tools or trackers.

## How we use it
To run Alto, keep accounts secure, fix bugs, answer feedback, and prevent abuse, and, only if you opt in, to read Bass conversations to improve Bass. If you are in the EU or UK, our legal bases are providing the service you asked for and our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and working.

## Who handles it for us
- **Google**: Firebase sign-in, database and hosting; Cloud Functions, storage, logging and secret storage; Google Forms for feedback.
- **Anthropic**: Bass only. Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days; inputs its safety systems flag can be kept up to 2 years. Under its commercial terms Anthropic does not train its models on content sent through its API. Anthropic may process requests outside the United States.
- **AI tools**: If you opt in, we may read your saved Bass conversations with AI tools.
They process your data for us under their own terms. Your content is stored in the United States. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

## Storage on your device
Alto sets no cookies. (Google's sign-in window sets Google's own cookies on Google's domain.) To work, Alto keeps in your browser: your sign-in session; your account details so it can show who is signed in (name, email, photo link); your settings (such as look, view and background); and copies that help it open faster (your page list and pages, files you attach while editing, and backgrounds you import). We use these only to run the site and keep your choices, so there is no cookie banner. You can clear them in your browser settings; you will be signed out and your settings reset.

## How long we keep it
Your content stays until you or we delete it. Email lukebmandel@gmail.com from your Google account address to ask for a copy or deletion. We aim to finish within 7 days and always within 30. We keep no scheduled backups of tester data during the beta, and any manual copy we make is deleted within 30 days. Logs are kept for 30 days. Records of who opened saved Bass conversations are kept for 400 days.

## Your choices and rights
You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. If you live in a US state with a privacy law, you have the rights that law gives you, and we will not treat you worse for using them. If you are in the EU or UK you can also object to or restrict processing and complain to your data protection authority. During the beta Alto is offered to testers in the United States only.

## Children
Alto is for people 18 and over and is not directed to children. We ask you to confirm you are 18 or over when you first sign in. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we will delete it the same day.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No system is perfect. If a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

## Do Not Track
We do not track you across other websites, so there is nothing to change when your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

## Changes
We will date every change and update this policy before Alto opens to the public.
