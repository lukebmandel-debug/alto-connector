# PMA Building Palace data: sources and licence notes

`pma_plan.json` is generated by `docs/building-palace/build_pma_plan.py` from public sources. Everything is sourced (a `src` id with its licence) or assumed (confidence 0-1 and a basis).

| source | use | licence / terms |
|---|---|---|

| OpenStreetMap way 28533972 | exterior shell outline; registration of the 1928 plates | ODbL 1.0, (c) OpenStreetMap contributors. Attribution is in the plan and the blueprint footer. A published database derived from it stays ODbL; replace by the City of Philadelphia footprint layer to drop the obligation |
| PMA visitor map, PMA event floor plans (2018), PMA press releases 2021, PMA library guide | gallery numbers, levels, collection areas, published dimensions, installation names (facts only) | (c) Philadelphia Museum of Art. Facts only; never traced, copied or redistributed |
| USGS 3DEP | grade for the terrace and steps | public domain |
| Wikimedia Commons photographs of the Great Stair Hall | viewed for height/material estimates | CC BY-SA / CC BY, not redistributed |
| Wikimedia Commons reproductions of 4 paintings (Peale, Tanner, Monet, Van der Weyden) (`pma_art.json`) | 512 px JPEGs in `alto/assets/bldg/pma/`, hosted under `/bart/` | public domain (artists d. 1464-1937; Commons licence label read 2026-10-19) |

Never used: images of works by Duchamp, Brancusi, Rivera, Calder or living artists (title cards only, no image); PMA photographs; the visitor map or event plans as drawings.
Withheld on purpose: the Leyster image (gallery 364 is confirmed but no traced room contains its label point), the Gross Clinic image (its gallery is 255 or 211 depending on the source), works whose gallery number is unconfirmed (Large Bathers, Sunflowers, Prometheus Bound), the Large Glass (PMA record: not on view).

Levels are the visitor map's G, 1, 2, 3. The 1928 sheets are basement = 1, first floor = 2, second floor = 3; G is not on them.

## 2026-10-21 realism pass (what is evidenced and what is assumed)

- **More art (36 hosted images):** 31 public-domain paintings added. Each was found through Wikidata (property P4643, PMA work ID, with a Commons image and an artist who died before 1956). Its gallery is the "Gallery NNN" the PMA object page stated on 2026-10-21. Its Commons file licence was read from the Commons API (`pd`; files marked CC BY / CC BY-SA were skipped). Each image is downscaled to 512 px (`sips`, JPEG q68). Sizes come from Wikidata (P2048/P2049), otherwise they are marked "assumed". Three works in Gallery 364 found no free wall and are listed as unplaced, with no image hosted. Tanner's *Annunciation* moved from 211 to 358 to match its current object page.
- **Assumed, not measured:** period-room finishes (`PERIOD_FINISH` in the generator: oak, pine, painted or white-and-gold panelling, parquet), the East Entrance vestibule's stone floor, vitrines in decorative-arts, Asian and arms galleries, and rope barriers in the period rooms. Also assumed: the outdoor entablature proportions, the Ionic capital detail (the order is from published descriptions), Diana's facing, and the absence of drapery on Diana. The Great Stair Hall stone is rendered as Kasota (Minnesota dolomite: "honey-coloured" per published descriptions).
- **Placeholders:** a work with no hosted image shows a plain panel with its title and maker and "image not shown". Tapestries show a plain woven ground. Nothing is invented.

## 2026-10-23 inferred doorways (every room on foot)

The traced 1928 plates leave rooms with no way in: the level 2 and 3 pavilions, most of level 3's north wing, the East Entrance vestibule and lobbies, and the whole of level G. On foot, only 79 of the 166 rooms could be reached from the Great Stair Hall. `INFERRED_DOORS` in the generator adds 25 doorways in 20 places (confidence 0, each with its basis in `bs`; 9 stand in short new partitions where no wall was traced). Each sits on a wall the two rooms share, centred on it unless a traced opening gives its place. None goes through a wall with the outside beyond it. The evidence used:
- **Traced "windows":** 2.0-2.4 m openings through a wing's east wall where a pavilion's passage or stair hall meets it, read by the tracer as windows.
- **Mirror image:** a doorway the 1928 plan shows at the mirrored place in the other wing.
- **Gallery sequence:** consecutive PMA gallery numbers in one row or column.
- **Room use:** an office onto its corridor, two halves of one 1928 room.

Also: level G's own doorways were being pruned (its rooms were never checked as room boundaries), and the Forum stair landed in the void above the Forum. It is now one straight flight that lands on level 1 past the Forum's north edge, with its position still assumed. `tests/fpv_walk/reach_audit.js` checks that every room stays reachable.
