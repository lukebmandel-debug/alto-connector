Alto — your home for everything you study and build. Each project lives in its own glass slab; each tile opens its own workspace.

Projects & tiles — open a live tile to enter it. Dimmed tiles are coming soon. Inside any workspace, the Alto mark at the top returns you here.

Search — the magnifier searches everything across Alto: projects, timelines, and the content inside them (scenes, characters, themes). Results jump straight to the right spot, even deep inside a timeline.

Reports — every report you generate inside a course is saved automatically. The small document bubble on a course tile opens that course's report repository.

Account — the person bubble at the bottom lefttile at the top left signs you in with Google so your work can follow you.
