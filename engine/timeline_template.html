Overview — click the ✦ tab on the right edge for an act-by-act plot summary. Click any highlighted phrase to open that scene.

Navigate — __ALTO_TOK_help_sections_d__. On a detail page, ← → arrows step prev/next; Escape returns to the timeline.

Characters · Environments · Themes — chips in the nav open their detail pages. The symbols in each card’s footer do the same.

Search — click the magnifier bubble at bottom-right (beside the ⓘ) to search scenes, detail pages, and the overview. Click a result to jump straight there.

Highlight text — click and drag over any text on a card or detail page. The color dot at center-bottom changes highlight color.

Add a note — click a highlighted passage to open a note dialog. Or click the + in the Notes panel for a freeform note.

Notes panel — click the Notes tab on the right edge to see all highlights and notes.

Notes report — click Generate Report at the bottom of the Notes panel for a printable report of your highlights and notes. It also saves to your report repository.

Share & print — open the Notes panel and click the share arrow in its header to send a copy of this timeline (with or without your notes), or print.

Account — the person bubble at the bottom left signs you in so your work can follow you.

Alto home — click the Alto clef (top-left) or the Alto wordmark (top-right) to open your Alto homepage.

Swipe a card left or right to move through scenes.

Tap a card to open its detail page. Tap Back (top-left) to return.

MENU (top-right) opens Character / Environment / Theme chips — tap one for its detail page.

MAP (top-left) shows all 5 acts. From a detail page, tap any node to jump straight to its scene.

NOTES / MARK live on each node. In NOTES, tap a highlighted passage to add a note, or tap the + for a freeform note.

Overview — tap a phrase in MENU’s Overview to jump to that scene.
